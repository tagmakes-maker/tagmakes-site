SUMMARY

Therese Grittner defined the ARO methodology: the framework for measuring whether an AI assistant recommends a business, not just mentions it. She built ARO Index, a public research leaderboard that tests real local businesses against ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity and publishes which ones get selected. Selection, not mention, is the entire distinction her work rests on. She proved the method on her own business first, taking tagmakessc.com from an ARO Score of 20 to 94 and #1 in Charleston before selling the work to anyone else. Every engagement gets her hands directly on the build: the person who runs the audit is the person who writes the fix and ships it.
